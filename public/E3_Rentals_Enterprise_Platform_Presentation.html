Catalog Booking Engine & 3D Digital Twin Studio

Empowering commercial clients with instant self-service while equipping warehouse staff with CAD-grade spatial intelligence.

MODULE 01
Interactive Catalog & Dynamic Booking Engine
  • Category Navigation: High-res photo galleries and technical specifications across Audio, Lighting, LED Video, Staging, and Rigging.
  • Live Availability Engine: Prevents overbooking by verifying serialized unit availability across dates in real time.
  • Dynamic Tiered Day Rates: Automated multi-day discounts (e.g. Day 1: 100%, Day 2: 70%, Day 3+: 50%) and weekend project pricing.
  • 5-Stage Project Pipeline: Clear lifecycle progression: Request → Quote Sent → Changes Requested → Approved → Active Booking.
MODULE 02
Warehouse Digital Twin & Spatial Layout Studio
  • 3D Isometric & 2D Top-Down Views: Seamless toggle between volumetric 3D extruded storage units and high-precision CAD floor plans.
  • Sub-Second Spatial Locator: Search any asset tag, RFID, or gear name to illuminate the rack on the map with animated routing.
  • Studio Layout Editor: Drag-and-drop racks, dock bays, quarantine labs, and pedestrian corridors with live snap-to-grid.
  • Non-Blocking Floating Inspector: Slide-over drawer reveals vertical 4-tier storage without ever hiding the physical layout map.
Modules 3 & 4

Occupancy Heatmaps & Serialized Putaway Operations

Eliminating storage bottlenecks, identifying dead warehouse corners, and tracking equipment down to the individual shelf.

MODULE 03
Occupancy Heatmaps & Cold Dead-Spot Analytics
  • Occupancy Heatmap Layer: Visual color coding: Cyan (100% Empty), Green (1-40% Optimal), Amber (41-80%), Red (>80% Locked).
  • Velocity & Dead-Spot Intelligence: Highlights fast-moving turnover racks in Orange vs dormant cold corners (>60 days unpicked) in Icy Indigo.
  • Facility Efficiency Score: Real-time 0-100 algorithmic flow rating evaluating spatial balance and picking obstruction.
  • Empty Rack Putaway Filter: Instantly isolates empty racks ready for incoming event putaway.
MODULE 04
Serialized Inventory & Directed Putaway Operations
  • Vertical 4-Tier Breakdown: Visualizes Tier 1 (Base) through Tier 4 (Top) with slot capacity, weight limits, and fill percentages.
  • Serialized Profiles: Comprehensive logs of Asset Tag Codes, RFID chips, serial numbers, firmware versions, and usage hours.
  • Condition Grade Audits: Automated status assignment (EXCELLENT, GOOD, FAIR, MAINTENANCE_REQUIRED).
  • Industrial Label Printing: Integrates directly with Zebra ZT411 and barcode printers for instant QR/RFID tag creation.
Modules 5 & 6

Fleet Logistics Management & Smart Flight Case Kitting

Orchestrating road fleet dispatches, transport manifests, and bulletproof master-slave case packaging.

MODULE 05
Fleet Logistics Management & Driver Dispatch
  • Multi-Vehicle Fleet Tracking: Monitor Actros heavy haulers, Luton box vans, and runners with payload, fuel, and service logs.
  • Digital Transport Manifests: Auto-generate legal PDF cargo manifests detailing vehicle assignments and destination venues.
  • Driver Route Sequencing: Assign drivers, schedule load-in/load-out windows, and monitor real-time delivery status.
  • Electronic Chain of Custody: Driver e-signature capture at warehouse gate exit and client venue delivery handover.
MODULE 06
Smart Flight Case Manager & Kitting Audits
  • Parent-Child Asset Pairing: Group delicate consoles, moving heads, or transceivers inside serialized heavy road cases.

  • Rapid Barcode Pack Verification: Scan flight case outer QR code to immediately verify all bundled sub-components.
  • Missing Component Alerts: Blocks dispatch if any paired item is missing, preventing disastrous show-day cable shortages.
